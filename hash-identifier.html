Hash Identifier

Identify unknown hash strings. Supports MD5, SHA family, bcrypt, Argon2, JWT, and more.

Hash identification is heuristic. Multiple algorithms share the same output length (e.g. MD5 and NTLM are both 128-bit / 32 hex chars). Confidence levels reflect how unique the signature is: high medium low
All processing happens locally in your browser. No hashes are sent anywhere.
